FAQ

Questions Aspirants Ask First

What is SSB?

SSB is a multi-day officer selection process that assesses psychology, group performance, communication and personal interview readiness.

Who can join?

NDA, CDS, AFCAT, Navy and other defence-entry aspirants can join according to their eligibility and goal.

Which exams do you prepare for?

We prepare aspirants for NDA, CDS, AFCAT, Navy and SSC / other officer-entry pathways.

What is the course duration?

Current course formats include 21-day foundation, 21-day communication and 2-day refresher tracks.

Is the course online or offline?

Mentoring can be structured for online and classroom-style practice depending on the batch.

What study material is included?

Students get TAT, WAT, SRT, SDT, OIR, PPDT, current affairs and interview preparation material.

Do you provide personal interviews?

Yes, interview practice and PIQ-based feedback are part of the structured mentoring process.
