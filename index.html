FAQ

Questions Aspirants Ask First

What is SSB?

SSB is a multi-day officer selection process that assesses psychology, group performance, communication and personal interview readiness.

Which SSB goals are available?

Current SSB goal options are ACC, SCO, SL and TA.

What courses are available?

Current course formats listed on the site are 21 Days SSB Foundation, 21 Days Communication, 2 Days Refresher, Personal Interview with 3× Interview Sessions and Pre-Recorded SSB Course.

What is the course duration?

The listed durations include 21 Days for Foundation, 21 Days for Communication, 2 Days for Refresher and 3× sessions for Personal Interview. Pre-recorded access duration is to be provided by client.

Is the course online or offline?

Course delivery mode is to be provided by client.

What study material is included?

Study material categories include TAT Story Sets, WAT Response Builder Sets, SRT Sets, OIR, Defence Current Affairs Notes and Lecturette Topic & Material to Prepare.

Do you provide personal interviews?

Yes. The listed Personal Interview course includes 3× Interview Sessions.
