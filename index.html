FAQ

Questions Aspirants Ask First

What is SSB?

SSB is a multi-day officer selection process that assesses psychology, group performance, communication and personal interview readiness.

Which SSB goals are available?

Preparation can be tailored to the entry you are aiming for across the Army, Navy or Air Force. Share your target entry and eligibility with a mentor to discuss a suitable preparation plan.

What courses are available?

Preparation focuses on SSB selection, including psychology tests, GTO tasks, personal interview readiness, current affairs and communication. Contact the mentors for current course and batch details.

What is the course duration?

Duration depends on the course and your preparation needs. Contact the mentors for current schedules and duration options.

Is the course online or offline?

Course format can depend on the current batch. Contact the mentors to confirm whether online or in-person options are available.

What study material is included?

Listed study material includes TAT Story Sets, WAT Response Builder Sets, SRT Sets, OIR, Defence Current Affairs Notes and Lecturette material. Contact the mentors to confirm what is currently available with each course.

Do you provide personal interviews?

Personal interview readiness is part of the mentor guidance. Contact the team to ask about mock interview format and availability.
